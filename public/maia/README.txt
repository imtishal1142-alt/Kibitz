Maia chess networks (Lc0 format), one per rating band.
Source: https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess (maia_weights/), licence GPL-3.0.
McIlroy-Young, Sen, Kleinberg, Anderson. "Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior: Chess as a Model System", KDD 2020.
The ratings are on the Lichess scale. They are played here by a small JavaScript runner (src/lib/engine/lc0net.ts), one forward pass per move, no search.
